## Notícia

## Paralelo histórico
2010: a senadora Lisa Murkowski perdeu a primária do partido, concorreu como write-in (nome escrito à mão), mais de 100 mil cédulas conferidas uma a uma; venceu por ~10 mil votos, primeira eleita por write-in desde Strom Thurmond (1954); resultado certificado em 30/12/2010.

## Narração (132 palavras)

Days after Election Day, Alaska's Senate race was still undecided. Fewer than a thousand votes separated the top two candidates.

Why so slow? Ballots are flown or mailed in from remote corners of the state, and Alaska uses ranked-choice voting. That final tabulation can't happen before November eighteenth.

Alaska has done slow counts before.

In 2010, Senator Lisa Murkowski lost her party's primary and ran as a write-in. Her supporters had to write her name on the ballot by hand.

Election workers reviewed more than a hundred thousand write-in ballots, one by one. It took more than two weeks to call the race.

She won by about ten thousand votes, the first senator elected by write-in since Strom Thurmond in 1954.

In Alaska, patience is part of the ballot. The American File.
