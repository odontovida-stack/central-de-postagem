The two-party system isn't in the Constitution, and no federal law requires it. Washington warned against "the spirit of party" in 1796. Yet for more than 170 years, every president has come from the same two parties. Here's how winner-take-all math, ballot rules, money, debates and district maps keep it that way. Not a left or right video: every example of one party gaming the rules is paired with one from the other side.
