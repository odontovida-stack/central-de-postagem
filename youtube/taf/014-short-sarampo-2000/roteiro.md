## Narração

Global health authorities say the United States has lost its measles elimination status.

That means the virus has spread here continuously for more than twelve months.

This year, America has recorded more than five thousand measles cases. Last year's total was already the highest in more than three decades.

Before a vaccine arrived in 1963, measles infected an estimated three to four million Americans every year. About forty-eight thousand were hospitalized. Four to five hundred died, most of them children.

Vaccination drove those numbers down. In 2000, the United States declared measles eliminated.

That status held for more than twenty-five years.

Now, for the first time since 2000, America no longer meets that standard.

The American File.
