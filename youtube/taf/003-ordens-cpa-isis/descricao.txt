In May 2003, the U.S.-led Coalition Provisional Authority in Iraq signed two short orders. CPA Order 1 banned senior members of Saddam Hussein's Baath Party from public jobs. CPA Order 2 dissolved Iraq's army, intelligence services and Ministry of Defense. In June 2014, a group calling itself the Islamic State declared a "caliphate" across parts of Iraq and Syria.

This file follows the chain between those two dates: the case made for the orders and the warnings against them, the insurgency and Zarqawi's strategy of sectarian war, Camp Bucca, the surge and the Awakening, the 2011 withdrawal debate, Prime Minister Maliki's crackdown on Sunni protests, Syria's civil war, and the fall of Mosul.

Several key numbers are disputed (how many people lost their jobs, how many former officers ended up in ISIS leadership). Where sources disagree, the video gives the range and says who estimated what.

This video is history plus commentary. The narrator's opinions are clearly marked as opinion. Every quote is real and attributed, and the sources are listed below.
