On November 5, 2026, California Gov. Gavin Newsom said he would commute all 548 death sentences in the state to life without parole. More than half of the cases still need approval from the California Supreme Court. California emptied its death row once before: in February 1972, when its Supreme Court struck down the death penalty, only for voters to restore it that November.
