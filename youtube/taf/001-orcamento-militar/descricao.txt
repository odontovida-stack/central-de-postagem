The US will spend about $1 trillion on national defense this year: ~$901 billion in the regular FY2026 budget plus ~$113 billion from the 2025 reconciliation law. Where does it go, who gets it, and does it make Americans safer?

In this file:
• How America went from a tiny peacetime army to a permanent war economy
• Eisenhower's 1961 warning about the military-industrial complex
• What the money actually buys: troops, F-35s, carriers and a ~$946 billion nuclear bill
• The five contractors that took $771 billion in Pentagon contracts (2020–2024)
• ~750 bases abroad and who pays for them
• The outcomes record, and why Congress never cuts the budget
• Eight failed Pentagon audits in a row

Every number in this video comes from public sources. The commentary is the opinion of The American File. This video uses an AI voice and AI-assisted research; every figure was checked against the sources below.
