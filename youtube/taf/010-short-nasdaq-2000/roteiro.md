## Narração

On November fifth, the Nasdaq fell more than five percent in a single day. Its worst day since April 2025.

Chip stocks were hit hardest. The Philadelphia semiconductor index dropped more than twelve percent. Nvidia lost about nine.

The trigger? Investors feared new limits on data centers after the midterm elections.

The Nasdaq closed more than ten percent below its October record. That's a correction.

America has seen a tech boom crack before.

March 10, 2000: the Nasdaq peaked above five thousand, riding the internet boom.

By October 2002, it had lost more than three quarters of its value.

It took fifteen years to get back to that record.

One bad day is not a crash. The next day, the Nasdaq bounced back about one percent.

History's lesson: watch the trend, not the day. The American File.
