## Narração

On Friday, November 13th, the FAA ordered a ground stop at every airport in the United States.

For about four hours, departures across the country were frozen.

Thousands of flights were canceled. Thousands more were delayed.

The FAA blamed a telecommunications outage affecting air traffic control centers. Officials said there was no sign of a cyberattack.

Only one nationwide ground stop has lasted longer.

September 11th, 2001. For the first time ever, the FAA halted all air traffic. Within an hour, all U.S. airspace was closed.

Airlines weren't allowed back in the sky until September 13th.

The last nationwide stop before this one came in January 2023, when a damaged file in a key safety system grounded flights for about an hour and a half.

This time, the cause was equipment, not an attack. The American File.
