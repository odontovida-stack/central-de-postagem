## Narração

On Thursday, November 12th, Outlook went dark for users around the world.

Microsoft 365 services tied to email, including Outlook, Teams and Copilot, were disrupted.

Within about an hour, outage reports came in from 74 countries and territories.

More than twelve hours later, Microsoft said it had only marginal success restoring service. The company said there was no sign of a cyberattack.

Microsoft says more than 430 million people use its 365 apps.

It's not the first time one glitch shook the world's computers.

July 19th, 2024. A faulty CrowdStrike software update crashed about 8.5 million Windows devices. That was less than one percent of Windows machines worldwide.

Even so, flights were grounded, and hospitals, banks and retailers were disrupted.

When so much runs on the same software, one bad change can stop the world. The American File.
