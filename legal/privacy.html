Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026 (affiliate links section added)

"Central de Postagem" is a private publishing tool used only by its owner, Luiz Alexandre Molina Soares (Lins, SP, Brazil), to schedule and publish his own content to his own accounts on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube (Brasil 23:59, Brazil on a Budget, Esportes na Tela, Jogos na Tela, Tudo o que Estreia, Lançamentos de Games, Shows Confirmados, The American File and Before History Changed). It is not offered to the public.

What data the tool accesses

  • Basic profile information of the owner's own accounts (username, account ID), needed to publish.
  • The content the owner chooses to publish (images, videos, captions).
  • Public comments on the owner's own posts, only to answer keyword requests (for example, someone commenting "GUIDE" receives the link to the guide).
  • Aggregate statistics of the owner's accounts (followers, reach, likes), for the owner's own reports.

Affiliate links

Some links on our pages are affiliate links (Amazon, Payhip and travel partners such as GetYourGuide and Viator through Travelpayouts). The Brazil on a Budget link page loads a Travelpayouts script that turns travel links into affiliate links and may set cookies to credit the referral. We may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not collect data from people who do not interact with the owner's posts.
  • We do not use the data for advertising.

Storage and retention

Access tokens are stored encrypted as GitHub Actions secrets and are never published. The only personal data kept is the ID of comments already answered, so the same person is not answered twice; it is deleted after 90 days.

Your choices

To have your data removed, or for any question about this policy, write to kko.molina@hotmail.com. We reply within 7 days.
